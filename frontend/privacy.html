Privacy Policy & Data Security Notice

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Biometric Data Handling

This application NEVER collects, receives, or stores raw biometric data (such as fingerprint images, facial recognition scans, or iris data). All biometric verification is handled locally on your smartphone operating system (iOS TouchID/FaceID, Android Biometric Prompt, or Windows Hello) via standard WebAuthn / Passkey public-key cryptography.

2. Geolocation Collection

GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, and accuracy) are requested only when you actively trigger an authentication/attendance attempt. Location data is used strictly to compute server-side distance to authorized areas and record compliance in the attendance log. Background tracking is never performed.

3. Data Retention

Authentication logs (timestamp, distance, GPS accuracy, result) are retained for audit and institutional record-keeping according to administrator settings.

4. Access Control

Attendance logs are accessible only to authorized system administrators and to the individual user for their own personal attendance history.
